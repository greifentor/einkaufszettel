# Architecture 

This document contains architecture descriptions and documentation.


## Introduction & Goals

### Requirements Overview

* Maintenance of shops, products and mandators.
* Maintain sort orders of products in shops.
* Shopping list maintenance (add and confirm products).
* Backup and restore of mandator data.


### Quality Goals

* SOLID
* TDD
* Minimal amount of code.


### Relevant Stakeholders

* The Developers.


## Architecture Constraints

* Clean Architecture (hexagonal architecture).
* Trying a domain and transaction based implementation.
